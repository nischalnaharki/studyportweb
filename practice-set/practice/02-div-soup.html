Getting around Kathmandu by bus

Popular routes

Ratnapark → Koteshwor

Every 5 minutes, 6 AM to 8 PM. About 25 minutes.

Kalanki → Chabahil (Ring Road)

Sajha Yatayat buses every 10 minutes.

Tip

Keep small change ready; conductors rarely have change for Rs 500.

Fares

Most city routes cost Rs 20–30. Students pay less with an ID card.
